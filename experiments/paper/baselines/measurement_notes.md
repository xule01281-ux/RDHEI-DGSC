# Table 10 implementation and accounting

The capacity kernels are independently written from Ou and Zhao (2020), DOI 10.1109/TCSVT.2019.2921812, and Wahed and Nyeem (2025), DOI 10.1016/j.aej.2025.02.088. They are not the original authors' released codecs. No full extraction/reconstruction of these two comparators was performed.

HC-MHM uses the floored diamond predictor in Eq. 16, the 13-pixel/15-difference Fig. 5 template, 16 approximately equal-population histograms and monotone parameters within delta 1..6. Its second layer uses the first layer's actual modified image. Parameters maximize measured capacity under the candidate constraints rather than minimizing distortion at a specified fixed payload. The table uses the largest seed-0 gross capacity over these six deltas; seeds 1 and 2 are stored as additional checks.

I-DPVO uses three rounds, horizontal/vertical/horizontal, stable sorting, consecutive disjoint three-pixel groups, no processing of incomplete trailing groups, and pairing adjacent eligible extrema. Boundary preprocessing is repeated each round. The negative branch of Eq. 4b is interpreted symmetrically as `< -1`, correcting the apparent printed `> -1` condition. The reported figure is the cumulative gross count at round 3 with seed 0. These implementation conventions must be retained when interpreting the table.

The gross reference waives all auxiliary costs in both chessboard layers or all three sorting rounds. The proposed method's entry is instead actual net image payload after block/global metadata and displaced-byte overhead. The 53-byte external public header is reported separately. These values support a capacity reference comparison, not a same-accounting net gain or a general ranking of plaintext rate-distortion performance.

Separate diagnostic scripts encode complete location maps with a static 32-bit binary arithmetic coder. Nonconstant maps pay 58 header bits; constant maps pay 21. HC-MHM records the processed layer's boundary map; I-DPVO records the full boundary map and two group-skip maps. No ideal entropy estimate replaces the recorded arithmetic length. Auxiliary strings are simulated with equal-length random placeholders rather than fully serialized into a reversible image stream.

The papers do not uniquely specify every auxiliary-map modeling and placement choice. In the diagnostic implementation, ciphertext map overhead exceeds the gross count. This does not prove zero net capacity for every possible implementation. I-DPVO's measured plaintext gross values also do not exactly reproduce its paper's Table 6. HC-MHM's Tiffany diagnostic exceeds the fixed 2044-LSB reserved boundary region; its accounting balance is not an implemented net payload. These diagnostic net estimates are therefore excluded from Table 10.

The archived ordinary ciphertext inputs are the same fixed AES-CTR pixel arrays used for the original comparison. Keys are not distributed. The supplied source image hashes identify the required plaintexts; `prepare_inputs.py` validates them before preparing local copies. This design permits exact forward capacity recomputation on the table's ciphertext inputs without distributing original-image credentials.
